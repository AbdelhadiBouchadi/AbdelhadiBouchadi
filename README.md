## Selected projects

### DentaFlow: dental clinic management software
Agenda, patient records, odontogram, acts catalogue, documents and statistics, with role-based access and automatic WhatsApp reminders that the clinic controls.
**Stack:** Next.js, tRPC, Drizzle, PostgreSQL. [Case study](https://abdelhadev.me/en/work/dentaflow)

### Ophtachusm: hospital management system
Patient and scheduling platform for a hospital ophthalmology department in Agadir. It replaced manual booking and was used by the department for several months.
**Stack:** Next.js, MongoDB, Prisma, Auth.js. The repository is private; details on the [portfolio](https://abdelhadev.me/en/work).

### [MeetAI: AI meeting platform](https://github.com/AbdelhadiBouchadi/meetai)
Live voice meetings with custom AI agents, with transcripts and summaries generated in the background.
**Stack:** Next.js 15, tRPC, Drizzle, Neon, OpenAI Realtime API, Inngest.

### [Yoga booking platform](https://github.com/AbdelhadiBouchadi/yoga-booking-app)
Studio management with waitlists, recurring lessons, cancellation windows, English and French support, and email confirmations.
**Stack:** Next.js 15, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Arcjet, AWS S3, Resend.

### [CICIDS2017: network anomaly detection](https://github.com/AbdelhadiBouchadi/Anomaly_Detection_CICIDS)
University project that separates normal traffic from simulated attacks, graded 99/100. Supervised classifiers with L1/L2 regularization and a TensorFlow autoencoder that flags anomalies by reconstruction error.
**Stack:** Python, TensorFlow, XGBoost, scikit-learn.
